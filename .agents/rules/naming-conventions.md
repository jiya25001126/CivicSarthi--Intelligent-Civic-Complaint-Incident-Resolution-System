# CivicSarthi Project & Naming Guidelines

These rules apply across all phases, code, tests, documentation, and UI components.

## 1. Product Name & Branding
- **Canonical Product Title**: **CivicSarthi — Intelligent Civic Complaint & Incident Resolution System**
- **Short Name**: **CivicSarthi** (used across UI headers, page titles, README, documentation, package names, logs, API metadata).
- **Canonical Tagline**: `"From Complaint to Resolution."`
- **Prohibited Terms**: Under no circumstances use "SamadhanAI" or "JanSahay" in new code, configurations, or UI.
- **CPGRAMS Context Description**: Describe the relationship accurately:
  > **"CivicSarthi: Intelligent decision-support and civic incident resolution layer for grievance redressal systems like CPGRAMS."**
- **Public Branding Requirement**: Remove the word "prototype" from public-facing product branding and descriptions.
- **Truthful Status**: Do not claim official government authorization, production CPGRAMS integration, or government ownership. Maintain accurate technical status.

## 2. Package Names
- Root: `civicsarthi`
- Frontend: `civicsarthi-frontend`
- Backend: `civicsarthi-backend`
- Mock CPGRAMS Service: `civicsarthi-mock-cpgrams`

## 3. Transparency & Operating Notice
- **Notice Text**:
  > `"CivicSarthi is an independent civic incident resolution platform. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by DARPG/CPGRAMS. Demonstration records are synthetic and do not submit directly to official government portals without authorization."`

## 4. Repository & Branching Conventions
- Canonical Remote: `https://github.com/jiya25001126/CivicSarthi--Intelligent-Civic-Complaint-Incident-Resolution-System.git`
- Remote identifier: `origin`
- Branches: `main`, `phase-N-<feature-or-phase-name>`
- Tags: `phase-N`
